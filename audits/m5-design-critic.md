# M5 design critic: Studio + Shorts templates

Inputs: 22 gallery stills, `clip60.mp4` (frames in `build/m5/clip_frames/`), `shorts.mp4`. `npx impeccable detect remotion/src`: 0 findings, but it ran as regex only (element rules skipped).

## Fix list (most severe first)

1. **High: `ui/MoveList.tsx` (Playback).** At 13 s the start position already lists 1.e4 through 9...b5, and 10.Nxb5 cxb5 is visible before it is played. Fix: render only plies up to the current one, keep 9 rows, and pad the empty rows.
2. **High: brilliant stamp lands on the wrong move (`board/Highlights.tsx`/Playback, `playback-*.png`, clip 44-46 s).** "!!" sits on b5 while the list highlights **cxb5**, so it reads as Black's recapture being brilliant. Fix: clear the stamp when the next ply starts, and hold the board on the beat ply for the reaction (section 8: freeze, react on frame 7).
3. **High: audio hierarchy is inverted (`mascot/MascotLayer.tsx:80`, `audio/Sfx.tsx`).** Blips play at gain 1 (file mean -20 dB). Beat sfx run at 0.5 gain on files with a -27 to -38 dB mean, so the oof starts at about -35 LUFS momentary, about 13 dB under the blips. Next to a -14.5 LUFS voice they will not be heard. Voice and music in the clip are -91 dB fixtures (`silent.mp3`, `bed_calm_01.mp3`), so voice-versus-music ducking is **unverified**. Fix: set blip gain to about 0.35, put beat sfx 6-10 LU under the voice, and re-render the clip with real TTS and a real bed before Gate B.
4. **High: Shorts type is Studio-sized (`compositions/ShortsVideo.tsx`).** At 390 px phone width the bubble text (about 28 px) shows at about 10 px, the "M1" label is tiny, and the hook (about 52 px) is replaced by captions after about 1 s. Section 6 says "bigger type". Fix: hook at 72 px or more, held until the first caption word; bubble text at 44 px or more; eval label at 36 px or more.
5. **Medium: KeyMoment is dead for 14 s (`scenes/KeyMoment.tsx`, `key_moment-*.png`, clip 27-41 s).** The right column below the label is empty, with no eval bar and no mascot reaction. The section 5 "alternative line" exists only as arrows. Fix: show the line as Plex Mono SAN rows (d7, Nc3, Nf6, Bg5) and highlight each row as its arrow plays.
6. **Medium: thumbnail arrow (`compositions/Thumbnail.tsx`, `thumbnail-*.png`).** The arrowhead is buried under the knight. At 320×180 it reads as a stray green stroke, and the 4×4 crop holds 5 pieces with a black pawn that is barely visible. Fix: draw arrows above the pieces, stop the head at the square edge, and make the shaft at least 24 px.
7. **Medium: TitleCard widow (`scenes/TitleCard.tsx`, `title-en.png`).** The title leaves "17)" orphaned on line 2. Fix: balanced wrapping (`text-wrap: balance`) or a measured max width.
8. **Medium: captions orphaned on full-width scenes (`ui/Captions.tsx`; Title, PlayerIntro).** Captions stay at x=1000 with nothing above them. Fix: on scenes with no board, align captions to the content edge (x=96).
9. **Medium: PlayerIntro content (`scenes/PlayerIntro.tsx`).** Section 5 asks for ratings, and none are shown. Only Morphy has a country. Fix: when data is missing, either drop the field on both cards or show the same field (for example "consulting pair").
10. **Medium: same mascot line twice in one video.** "Now that is a sacrifice." plays in ColdOpen (2-4 s) and again in Playback (45 s). This breaks section 8 ("never the same mascot line twice"). Fix: ColdOpen uses a different bank line, or no bubble.
11. **Low:** Verdict shows "92.0" (use "92"), "6...  Nf6" (use "6...Nf6??" in red), and "1–0" in Mono (use Condensed). The Shorts eval bar has no track, so at M1 it reads as a divider (add a `#262A33` track). The bubble is fixed to its final width, so it looks empty while typing. The cold open runs 5.3 s against the 6-10 s rule (fixture). "+3.0" is not aligned with the bar's x=96.

## Passes

Studio layout numbers (board, bar, column, margins, 9 rows, 8 px gaps). Tabular Plex Mono figures. Contrast about 6:1, no Studio text under 28 px. One accent per scene. No gradients, glows or emoji. Sentence case. Mascot clear of the board, bar and captions everywhere. **Devanagari in all 11 HI stills: matras, reph, nukta and chandrabindu intact, no tofu.** Shorts: hook in frame 0, bottom 340 / right 120 clear (mascot bottom about 1570, tight), owner-approved 864 board, loop frame matches. Motion: hard cuts, one element entering at a time, no piece overshoot, 6-frame blunder shake. Thumbnail text legible at 320×180 in both languages.
